What if your painting
could come to life?

Lawin Canvas combines traditional visual art with Augmented Reality — transforming a painting into something you can see, discover, and share. The canvas on your wall stays exactly as it looks. The life inside it only reveals itself when you look closer.

See it. Scan it. Watch it come alive.

How it works

  1. 01

    Get your artwork

    Choose a Lawin Canvas piece and bring it home — a premium painting, ready to hang.

  2. 02

    Scan the QR code

    Every artwork ships with a discreet QR code, linked to its own AR experience.

  3. 03

    Point your camera at the artwork

    No app to install. Your phone's camera recognizes the painting in seconds.

  4. 04

    Watch it come alive

    The still image moves, sounds, and breathes — then settles back into silence.
